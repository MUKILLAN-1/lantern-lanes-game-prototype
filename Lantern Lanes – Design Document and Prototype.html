One-page design document

Player fantasy and audienceYou are a night-walker carrying the last lantern, gathering sparks to bring the dawn. Audience: casual players aged 10 and up, on phone or laptop, who want a calm but tense 2–3 minute session.
GoalCollect 60 sparks before your light reaches zero.
Core loopAction: move the lantern to one of three lanes. Feedback: spark chime flash and “+” text, or a purple shake on a dark drop. Consequence: light rises or falls, combo grows or resets. Next decision: which lane to read next as drops speed up.
RulesLight starts at 100 and drains slowly. A spark gives +5 light and 1 point. Catching sparks in a row builds a combo (extra light every 5). A dark drop costs 20 light and resets combo. A missed spark resets combo only. Fall speed and spawn rate rise as you score.
Win, loss, restart, pause, onboardingWin: 60 sparks, dawn screen. Loss: light reaches 0. Restart any time (R or button). Pause with P, Esc or button. Start overlay explains the goal and controls before play.
ControlsKeyboard: ← / → or A / D move one lane; P or Esc pause; R restart. Touch: tap left or right half of the board, or use the on-screen buttons.
State modelFive states: start, playing, paused, win, lose. Allowed moves: start→playing, playing→paused, paused→playing, playing→win, playing→lose, win→playing, lose→playing. Restart is always a move to playing with a fresh reset. Any other move is ignored, so a key press can never put the game in an impossible state.
Scoring and progressionThree levels of 20 sparks each. A spark scores 10 × level points, and every fifth spark in a row adds 25. Level 1: slow drops, 30% dark. Level 2: faster, 35% dark. Level 3: fastest, 40% dark. Difficulty changes light drain and dark-drop damage: Easy 1.5/s and −15, Normal 2/s and −20, Hard 2.5/s and −25.
PersistencelocalStorage stores only difficulty, screen shake on or off, and one best score per difficulty. Data is validated on load, falls back to defaults if missing or corrupt, and can be cleared with one button. No personal data is stored.
Scope cutsNo audio, power-ups, enemies or accounts. Art is placeholder shapes drawn on canvas; all assets are original.
